## Primary contrast and sample

Two new adapter initializations, seeds 401 and 402, share the pinned pretrained
Qwen3-4B base. These are paired starting-state replications, not two independent
tasks or a population estimate. Train on the same four acquired entities, all
16 conditions. At 256 updates, then 512/1024 only if needed, require >=60/64
complete recall and >=28/32 complete cases on the original development entities.
Use the earliest passing checkpoint. Preserve every attempt and report failure
denominators if a seed never becomes ready. All data-order, LoRA and optimizer
settings remain fixed from development. Fresh evaluation never selects a state.

Fresh entities wexlun, pazdor, jivrek and qomset appear in no training, correction,
boundary or development-readiness check. Evaluate their 64 conditions alongside
64 familiar acquired and 64 target-pool conditions: 192 primary cases per state.
All formerly acquired instances remain visible, including two familiar entities
that never receive current-revision correction labels. No outcome-based subset.

Each seed starts novel and bridged arms from IDENTICAL acquired weights. At each
of two revisions, use 64 blocks of exactly one correction, one boundary and one
valid historical update (192 updates). Novel correction/boundary entities are
four novel entities; bridged are the first two familiar plus first two novel
entities. Conditions, labels, update order, optimizer resets and history match.
The intervention changes identity overlap in both correction and contrastive
examples. Token budgets are measured, not assumed identical from update counts.
History and correction validity are investigator supplied. At revision 2, eight
historical records affected by revision 1 are explicitly relabeled for rehearsal;
the eight newly obsolete revision-2 records are excluded. This additional support
is identical in both arms and is not autonomous validity inference.

Version 1 waives certification for copper/fast; version 2 also waives it for
violet/slow. The first waiver must remain effective after learning the second.
All orders start from their stated stock, independently reset. The output proposal
is executed atomically by the checker. This tests dependent eligibility,
reservation, shipment and final-state consequences, not open-ended planning,
identifier copying, cross-order inventory or query-driven learning.

## Queries and references

At each of versions 0/1/2, each candidate executes a full 192-order pass followed
by a fixed 32-order repeat batch: all conditions for daxen and wexlun (one familiar,
one fresh entity). Thus one candidate has 672 recurring use events. Primary
accuracy denominators use the full pass once; repeats establish reproducibility
and count toward actual use costs, not independent statistical evidence.
Development repeated every case twice and all repeats matched; reducing redundant
final repeat generations is a prospective resource choice, not sample selection.

The carried no-update reference generates the version-0 full pass and repeat
batch once. Because its prompts omit policy versions, those exact outputs are
rescored under versions 1/2 without new inference; explicitly mark that reuse.
The weak unadapted complete-rule prompt reference is evaluated once per version
on all 192 cases. Retain failures. The competent formal-policy interpreter executes
exactly the candidate's 672-order sequence, from versioned JSON policies. Its
privileged investigator-authored executable information and implementation cost
are disclosed; it is not example-equivalent or a neural editor reproduction.
