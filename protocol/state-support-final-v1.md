## Claim and challenge

Development suggests support benefit depends on inherited state: Novel support
benefits Novel-history weights but not Bridged-history weights. Test that
interaction on two fresh acquisitions, seeds501/502, not only new tickets.
Prospective directional expectation: the complete-success Novel-support benefit
is greater under Novel history than Bridged history. Compute, per seed,
(NN - NB) - (BN - BB), in counts out of 192; zero or negative challenges the
directional explanation. Also report both support effects and both state effects,
all four endpoints, and both waiver scopes. Do not require or predict a universal
192/192 cell. This is a finite-task assessment, not a population estimate.
