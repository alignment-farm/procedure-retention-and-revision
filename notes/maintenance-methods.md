The new task extends this study from call routing to checking eligibility,
consuming inventory and shipping with valid resulting state. Its overlap with
MQuAKE is evaluating consequences of an edit, not reproducing multi-hop factual
QA or MeLLo. Plain supervised LoRA with history is not WISE's routed side memory,
SEAL's learned self-edit generator, or LOKI's projected update. Explicit rules
are an investigator-maintained contextual policy reference, without retrieval.
Novelty is limited to this bounded causal workload and its measured outcomes.

The independent checker rejects omission of a required shipment, illegal
reservation, incorrect inventory and false state reports. The complete task is
one generated five-field plan executed atomically in a sandbox, not an agent
interacting with separate tools. Repeated use resets the sandbox per order;
there is no cross-order inventory depletion or online query learning. Removing
identifier copying intentionally tests whether earlier losses were confined to
string production. These scope choices must remain visible in publication.
